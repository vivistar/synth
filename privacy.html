1. Overview and Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how PRIME ISO Services, LLC ("PRIME ISO," "we," "us," or "our"), a limited liability company owned and operated by Terry M Patterson, approaches privacy in connection with The SaaS Preflight Check for Synthetic Users ("Service"). Because the Service is a fully client-side, static web application — meaning all processing occurs within your browser with no PRIME ISO backend server involved — our data practices are fundamentally different from conventional web services.

This Policy applies to users accessing the Service through PRIME ISO's own distribution — specifically the canonical repository at github.com/vivistar/synth and any hosting operated directly by PRIME ISO. Because the Service is released under the Apache License, Version 2.0, third parties may fork, modify, and redistribute their own versions. This Policy does not govern third-party forks or deployments. If you are using a version of the tool distributed by someone other than PRIME ISO, their privacy practices apply and PRIME ISO bears no responsibility for them.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Due to the client-side architecture of the Service, PRIME ISO does not collect, receive, store, or process the following categories of information:

  • Text you enter into any form field, textarea, or chat input;
  • API keys you optionally provide;
  • AI-generated outputs, persona prompts, or chat transcripts;
  • Assessment answers or wizard step selections;
  • IP addresses, device identifiers, or browser fingerprints;
  • Usage patterns, click events, or session recordings;
  • Names, email addresses, or any other personally identifiable information.

3. Client-Side Data Processing

All data you enter into the Service is processed exclusively within your browser's JavaScript environment. This includes:

  • Form inputs and configuration fields — retained in browser memory (JavaScript variables) only for the duration of your active session and discarded immediately when the tab is closed or refreshed.
  • API key fields — stored in a password-type DOM input element; never written to localStorage, sessionStorage, cookies, or IndexedDB.
  • Chat transcripts and AI outputs — maintained in a session-scoped JavaScript array (chatLogs) and erased on page reload. The "Export Chat" and "Download Report" features write files directly to your device without transmitting them to any server.
  • Wizard answers and governance scores — computed and rendered in-browser; no data leaves your device.

4. Third-Party AI Services (OpenRouter)

The Service routes AI requests through OpenRouter (openrouter.ai) to access large language models such as openai/gpt-4o-mini. When AI features are used:

  • On PRIME ISO's canonical Vercel deployment, prompts are forwarded from your browser to a PRIME ISO-operated server-side proxy, which then transmits them to OpenRouter over HTTPS. PRIME ISO does not log or store prompt content on its proxy.
  • If you supply a custom OpenRouter API key, prompts are transmitted directly from your browser to OpenRouter, bypassing the proxy entirely. PRIME ISO does not receive or intercept those requests.
  • OpenRouter may in turn forward prompts to third-party model providers (e.g., OpenAI). Each provider's privacy policy governs their handling of prompt data.
  • If you use a Custom API Key, all charges and data handling obligations associated with that key are governed by your agreement with OpenRouter and the applicable model provider.

We strongly recommend reviewing OpenRouter's Privacy Policy before submitting sensitive business data as prompt content.

The Usability Tester feature supports image upload and paste. Any image you provide is encoded as a base64 data URI and included in the prompt payload sent to OpenRouter. Images are not stored by PRIME ISO.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Service does not set first-party cookies, and PRIME ISO does not deploy any analytics, advertising, or behavioral tracking scripts. The application loads the following third-party CDN resources for functionality and visual rendering only:

  • Tailwind CSS via cdn.tailwindcss.com — for styling;
  • Lucide Icons via unpkg.com — for iconography;
  • Google Fonts (Inter) via fonts.googleapis.com — for typography;
  • Perplexity logo (Lifecycle Rationale view) — rendered from a static CDN URL.

These CDN providers may log standard HTTP request metadata (IP address, user agent, referrer) in accordance with their own policies. PRIME ISO does not receive or have access to these logs.

6. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has used the Service in a way that raises privacy concerns, please contact us.

7. Data Security

Because PRIME ISO does not receive or store user data, our security obligations are centered on the integrity of the application code itself. We commit to:

  • Maintaining the Service as a static HTML file with no hidden data exfiltration pathways;
  • Using HTTPS for all outbound API calls;
  • Not embedding third-party scripts beyond the CDN resources listed in Section 5;
  • Conducting periodic reviews of the application's network activity for unintended data transmission.

8. Your Rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data, most traditional data subject rights (access, deletion, portability) are satisfied by default — there is simply no data held about you to act upon. If you have concerns about data processed by OpenRouter or an underlying model provider on your behalf, please refer to their respective privacy controls and data deletion tools.

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or California may have additional rights under GDPR, UK GDPR, or CCPA respectively. Given that PRIME ISO collects no personal data, these regulations impose minimal obligations, but we remain committed to full compliance and welcome any inquiry.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be noted prominently. Continued use of PRIME ISO's distribution of the Service after a revision constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy. If you run a self-hosted or forked version, updates to this Policy do not automatically apply to your deployment — you are responsible for your own users' privacy under your own deployment.
