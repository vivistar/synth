Run the 8-gate pipeline by hand

Walk the pipeline, one gate at a time.

You don't need the tool. For staged teams, the 8-gate framework is a sequential checklist: at each gate ask the question, and a "no" routes you out to real people instead of letting an ungrounded synthetic run drive the decision. Answer all eight "yes" and you've earned a synthetic pass — still confirmed by humans at the ship point.

Ask at each gate — a "no" is where you stop
1
Do you have a clear, documented decision target?
Vague or exploratory → define the decision first; scope it down to one answerable question.
2
Is it low-to-medium risk and reversible?
Irreversible, or touches billing / contracts / security → reframe into smaller, reversible releases.
3
Is it a structured interaction or pricing-logic check?
Speculative or generative discovery → run human discovery first; synthetics can't find net-new needs. NN/g 2024
4
Do you have live customer data to ground it?
No telemetry / logs / interviews → real customers only; ungrounded synthetics hallucinate. Making Science 2025
5
Is the persona specific (role, rights, budget, expertise)?
Generic template → recalibrate; there's no testable scope until the persona is concrete and bounded.
6
Does the agent replicate a known past result?
No baseline, or drift > 15% → recalibrate; tighten prompts and re-test. MeasuringU 2026
7
Is a human + synthetic check-loop in place?
Synthetic-only → real customers only; a closed loop never disagrees with you. Codexical 2026
8
Are compliance tags and drift reviews defined?
No tagging → brainstorm only; untagged output isn't decision-grade. Koji 2026

Two gates are hard stops for humans

Gate 4 — no real data and Gate 7 — no human loop both route to real customers only. Grounding and human validation are the anchor; without either, a synthetic pass is just confident fiction.

Where a "no" routes you

Define the decision / reframe (gates 1–2) — the question is too loose or too risky. Shrink it.
Human discovery first (gate 3) — exploratory work needs live interviews, not simulation.
Real customers only (gates 4 & 7) — no grounding data, or no human loop.
Recalibrate (gates 5–6) — generic persona or failed baseline; fix and re-test.
Brainstorm only (gate 8) — untagged output; use for divergent ideas, not validation.

Why the gates exist

Synthetic users are fast but systematically over-agreeable, emotionally flat, and confidently wrong on decision-grade work. The gates force grounding (gate 4), calibration (gate 6), and a human loop (gate 7) so a synthetic pass is earned, not assumed. The research consensus: augment, never replace.
