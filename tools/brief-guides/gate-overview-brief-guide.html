The 8-Gate Framework — for teams building in defined stages.

If your team works in phases (discovery, design, build, QA) with handoffs and reviews, run every decision through these eight gates in order before you trust a synthetic-user result. Each gate is a checkpoint: pass it and advance, or fail it and get routed to real humans. The color tells you what kind of check it is and how hard it bites.
