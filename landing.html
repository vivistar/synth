Know when synthetic users help, and when they'll fool you.

Should you trust a synthetic user to change your roadmap? Synthetic personas can hallucinate or help. The SaaS Preflight Check is an interactive governance tool for SaaS product managers. Run your product decision through an 8-gate check before letting AI-simulated users influence your roadmap.

The Problem

Synthetic users are powerful — and easy to misuse.

AI-simulated user feedback can accelerate research by 10×. But when applied to the wrong decisions, they create feedback loops that distort product metrics, inflate conversion assumptions, and mislead entire roadmap cycles.

Most teams don't know where the line is — they either over-trust synthetic data or dismiss it entirely.

The Solution

An 8-gate governance check that tells you exactly where you stand.

Before running a synthetic user study, walk your decision through eight sequential gates — from risk assessment and data grounding to calibration, hybrid loops, and compliance labeling.

You get a clear verdict: proceed, adjust, or use real customers only.

Four Modules

Everything in one self-contained file.

No installation. No server. Open index.html in any browser and you're running.

Interactive Map

Visual 8-step pipeline. Click any gate to see its operational guide, trigger scenarios, and lifecycle rationale — what phase of the SaaS lifecycle it maps to and what agentic AI phase it serves.

Run Simulator

Step-by-step wizard with a yes/no choice at each gate. Navigate forward and back, watch the visual step tracker update, and receive a detailed pass / warn / fail verdict with a downloadable report.

AI Sandbox & Prompt Architect

Configure a SaaS persona with real baseline data, company size, and industry. OpenRouter generates a bias-resistant system prompt. Then interview the simulated user live and audit for calibration drift and sycophancy.

Lifecycle Rationale

A strategic positioning matrix — toggle between Standard Product Lifecycle and AI/Agentic Lifecycle to see where synthetic users genuinely help and where you must rely on real human feedback.

The 8 Gates

Every decision runs the same gauntlet.

Gates marked red are decision branches — fail one and you're routed to a safer path. Amber gates are calibration checks.
