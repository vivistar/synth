3. Description of the Service

The Anchorflight Check for Synthetic Users was designed to help product managers evaluate the appropriateness and reliability of synthetic user research within their product development and operations workflows. The Service includes:

  • An 8-step governance flowchart and interactive map;
  • A step-by-step wizard for evaluating product decision checkpoints;
  • An AI Sandbox and Prompt Architect for generating synthetic user personas;
  • A Usability Tester for AI-assisted interface evaluation using synthetic user perspectives, including support for image-based (screenshot) analysis;
  • A "How to Build Synthetic Users" guide with copy-paste prompt templates for product managers;
  • A Lifecycle Rationale view for strategic research positioning.

The Service integrates with the OpenRouter API (openrouter.ai) to route AI requests to supported large language models. By default, the Service uses openai/gpt-4o-mini. Users may optionally supply a custom OpenRouter API key to route requests through their own billing account. PRIME ISO does not operate or control the underlying AI models.
